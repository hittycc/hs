THE HS NEWS PAGE

13 September 2026

  • Shark fossils predate Saturn's rings. Sharks have existed on Earth for about 450 million years, while scientists estimate that Saturn's iconic rings formed much more recently, likely within the last 10 to 100 million years.
  • Clouds can weigh over a million pounds. A typical white, fluffy cumulus cloud is about one kilometer across and holds roughly 500,000 kilograms (1.1 million pounds) of water droplets suspended in the air.
  • The world's quietest room causes hallucinations. Microsoft built a concrete anechoic chamber in Washington that registers at -20.6 decibels. It is so silent that you can hear your own heartbeat, your lungs scraping together, and your ears ringing, which causes most people to hallucinate within 45 minutes.
  • Bananas are naturally radioactive. Because they are rich in potassium, bananas contain a small amount of the isotope potassium-40. Eating one exposes you to a tiny, completely harmless dose of radiation, a measurement sometimes playfully called the "Banana Equivalent Dose" by nuclear scientists.
  • Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Teaching at Oxford existed in some form by 1096, whereas the Aztec civilization is estimated to have begun with the founding of the city of Tenochtitlan later in 1325.
  • Wombat poop is shaped like a cube. The Australian wombat is the only known animal in the world that produces cube-shaped feces. Their intestines have unique horizontal ridges and varying elasticity that sculpt the waste, preventing it from rolling away so they can use it to mark their territory.
  • Cleopatra lived closer to the moon landing than the building of the Great Pyramid. Cleopatra died around 30 BC. The Great Pyramid of Giza was completed around 2560 BC (over 2,500 years before her life), while the Apollo 11 moon landing happened in 1969 AD (roughly 2,000 years after her death).
